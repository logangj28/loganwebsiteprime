My personal history with fantasy football

2017: Humble Beginnings

In 2017, my middle school friends and I started a father-son fantasy football league. We used primitive rules such as non-ppr scoring, but at the core of the league was pure joy and love for the game. We drafted based on who we liked or who espn recommended, not based off of who "experts" thought would do well. After getting Dalvin Cook stollen from us, my dad and I settled for Todd Gurley - who ended up winning us the league!

The Greatest League

In highschool, some of my middle school friends and some new highschool friends started what would go on the become The Annual. The league became known for its high level of trade activity. Seeing our leaguemates in school every day was a recipe for constant negotiations and chaos. After finishing in 2nd, 10th, and 11th during the first 3 years, I climbed to the mountaintop and won the league during Senior year's 4th Annual. That year of the league boasted 86 trades including the famous Puka Nacua trade between Henry and Paul, making it the most active league I have ever seen.

Present Day

Entering college, I took over as commisioner of The Annual, which is currently entering its 7th season. After a breif stint in the Field of Dreams league, I also joined Mills Fantasy and founded a UNC Fantasy football league. Today, that leaves me as a member of 1 league and a commisioner of 2. In the modern era of fantasy football, leaguemates have gotten sharper, AI has neutralized ball knowledge, and the fight to try and get an edge on the rest of the league has gotten more and more difficult. I am still seeking my third ever fantasy football championship.

Summary of my fantasy football career

In 8 years of playing fantasy football, I have participated in 12 fantasy football leagues, winning 2 times and making the playoffs 6 times. My most drafted player all time is Josh Allen. My best team was my 15-2 championship team in the 4th Annual, which consisted of Lamar Jackson, CMC, Tony Pollard, CeeDee Lamb, Justin Jefferson, TJ Hockenson, Jaylen Waddle, and Brandon Aubrey. My worst moment occurred during the 5th Annual, when I was eliminated from the playoffs with 2 minutes left in the final game of the fantasy regular season by a long touchdown from Joe Burrow to JaMmar Chase.
